# Handler interviews, September 2026: what they're unhappy about

Source: rook-wiki, Customer interviews database (console redesign research, run by the product designer). Four handlers, 2-5 Sep 2026. Each page is a transcript excerpt only. Keep handler names out of wide channels.

Interviewed: Aunt Dot (Vesper), Mr. Ambrose (Captain Vantage), Halloran (Sgt. Bulwark; also runs the gear cage), Kip (Meteor Mite and The Gale).

| # | Unhappy about | Raised by | Quote |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Callouts gone before the responder can answer | 3 of 4 (Aunt Dot, Ambrose, Halloran; Halloran only when asked at the end) | Aunt Dot: "by the time he's actually got a thumb on the screen — it's gone. Gone to somebody else already. And he just sort of stands there in the kitchen holding the phone." |
| 2 | Console alerts don't help the handler notice or tell apart a live callout | 3 of 4 (Aunt Dot, Ambrose, Kip) | Kip: "Mite and Gale both just go 'bing' and I have to actually look to know which one." |
| 3 | Text and badges too small to read at a glance | 2 of 4 (Aunt Dot, Ambrose) | Ambrose: "at a glance I sometimes cannot tell engaged from available without leaning in." |
| 4 | Uneven workload: some responders quiet, others swamped, no explanation | 2 of 4 (Aunt Dot, Kip) | Kip: "I just want it to look less like a coincidence when I'm staring at it at midnight and one card's dead quiet and the other's on fire." |
| 5 | No dark mode | 1 of 4 (Kip) | Kip: "the console is just this wall of white light in the middle of my face." |
| 6 | Filter resets silently | 1 of 4 (Ambrose) | Ambrose: "I'd rather it warned me the filter had reset than simply reset it." |
| 7 | Capability tag legend is buried | 1 of 4 (Ambrose) | Ambrose: "It sits in a drawer off the responder detail panel." |
| 8 | Supply problems, not console (requisition queue, no feedback on failure reports, weak catalog search) | 1 of 4 (Halloran) | Halloran: "I file a failure report and it goes into a void." |

## Notes
- Groups 1 and 4 are the same story from two sides; this matches the 4.2 data (load concentrated on 12 responders, four dropped out).
- No handler explains the cause; they describe symptoms only.
- Group 1 is the strongest signal. Ambrose's closest call: "halfway into the suit — coat on, one boot on — when it went to somebody else." Halloran dated his to "sometime in August".
- Group 6 is the filter-persistence noise; Ambrose is glad it shipped. Low priority.
- Group 8 belongs with the Supply 4.3 requisition approval chain work. Halloran also said the console is "fine" and he rarely uses it.
- Aunt Dot and Kip filed no support tickets, so these interviews are the only place their unhappiness shows up.
- Sofia's full notes are still missing from this folder; these are excerpts only.
