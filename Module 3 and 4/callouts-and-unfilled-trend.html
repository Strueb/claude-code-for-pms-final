Source: rook-database, tables callouts and pings. A callout counts as unfilled when none of its pings was taken. The unfilled share is the trailing 7-day total of unfilled callouts divided by the trailing 7-day total of callouts, shown once a full 7 days exist. Daily counts are small (0 to 6 unfilled), so read the line, not single days. No prior-year data exists, so a seasonal dip in callout volume cannot be ruled out; this chart only shows it does not account for the unfilled share. Hover or tab to a day for the values.
