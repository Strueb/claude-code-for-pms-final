How Dispatch decides who to ask, with a proposed retry after a miss

The routing code in 00-rook/code/dispatch-routing/ as it stands after 4.2 (12 Aug 2026). The purple dashed steps are a proposal (option G in the brief for Helen), not built.

The same flow as text
  1. A callout arrives with a region, a time, a location and the skills it needs.
  2. Dispatch finds everyone marked available in that region now (availability.py). It does not change anyone's availability.
  3. It scores each of them: 0.60 × travel-time closeness + 0.25 × recent-acceptance score + 0.15 × share of needed skills they have (routing.py, weights in config.py), and sorts best first. Nobody is removed.
  4. It takes the next responder on the list, pings their phone and waits up to 60 seconds (offer.py).
  5. If they take it, their score goes up 0.08 and the callout is filled.
  6. If they turn it down, their score goes down 0.12 and Dispatch moves on.
  7. If they don't answer in 60 seconds, the offer is withdrawn from their phone and recorded as missed.
  8. Proposed: on a responder's first miss for this callout, ping the same responder once more and wait another 60 seconds. The same three outcomes apply to the second try. It could be limited to the last responder on the list.
  9. On a miss that is not retried (today, every miss), their score goes down 0.12, the same as a turn-down, and Dispatch moves on.
  10. If there is nobody left, the callout is unfilled: the code returns nobody, with no retry of the list and no alert.

Drawn from reading the code, not from running it. availability.py, the device calls in offer.py and the travel-time estimate are placeholders in this copy, so what they do inside is not shown. Scores are held in history.py: between 0 and 1, starting at 0.5. A responder's score only changes when they are pinged. The retry is a proposal: in the replay data the next responder took 90 of 135 missed pings, 26 callouts ended unfilled right after a miss, and we can't tell whether a repeat ping would be answered. The proposal changes the flow only; it does not change how a miss is scored (that is option B).
